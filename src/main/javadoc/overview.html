The public contract of the Magid narrative engine.

Magid implements these interfaces; tagpacks (plugins that add MagidML tags) compile against them and never against the engine. Every type a pack may use lives under {@code com.zarterstein.magid.api}, so a pack's class loader can take exactly {@code com.zarterstein.magid.api.*} from the engine and everything else from the pack.

Version 0.x: the contract is being extracted from the engine, and minor versions may still break compatibility.
